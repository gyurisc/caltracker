# caltrack — privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-15_

caltrack is a personal calorie and training log used by one person: its author.
It has no other users, no accounts, no sign-up, and is not offered as a service
to anyone.

## What WHOOP data is read

With the owner's explicit authorisation through WHOOP's OAuth flow, caltrack
reads the following from the owner's own WHOOP account:

- sleep duration and sleep performance
- recovery score
- day strain
- resting heart rate and heart-rate variability
- workouts, including their energy estimate

No other WHOOP data is requested, and access can be revoked at any time from the
WHOOP app or account settings, which stops all further reads immediately.

## What happens to it

The data is written to a SQLite file on a personal computer in the owner's home
and is used only to annotate that person's own food log — for example, to mark
a day as a training day or a rest day.

It is **not** sent to any third party, shared, sold, published, used for
advertising, or used to train any model. The machine it lives on is not
reachable from the public internet.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it, which they can do at any time by
deleting the local database file. There is no backup held anywhere else.
